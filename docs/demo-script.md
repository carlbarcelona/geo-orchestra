# Demonstration script (about 4 minutes)

Start from a fresh browser, at full screen and in the foreground.

1. **Problem (20 s).** EIC visualizations are visual only. GeoOrchestra makes NASA rainfall audible *and* inspectable.
2. **Numerical → NASA sample.** "295 months of IMERG V07B over the Philippines, prepared from NASA's archive and validated (295/295 months, 5/5 spot checks)."
3. **Select a region.** Choose Mindanao in Pins / Regions, then open Map and click a cell near Manila: "a single cell versus an area mean, labelled differently."
4. **Musical sonification.** Press Space (the first click enables audio). "Higher pitch means wetter." Point at the shared cursor moving across the graph, table and map.
5. **Geophony.** Switch mode: the same measurements become rain density.
6. **Inspect.** Pause, click a note, open Event details: date, area mean, original mm/hr, note, mapping rain-v1.
7. **Honesty.** Load the Synthetic demo to show a missing month (a red gap with no note). Then load the NASA sample again.
8. **Visual feed.**
   1. Import the authentic SVS 5478 frames.
   2. Show that audio stays disabled until measurements are connected.
   3. Connect the climatology and play.
   4. Explain: "seasonal average, not a historical event; the linkage is a reconstruction; the frames include Feb 29, which NASA's own date list omits. We read every frame's date from its overlay."
9. **Close.** Audio legend, keyboard control and the accessibility options.

Fallback: a recording or screenshots, clearly labelled as such.
