The `rain-v2` hum, the chords and the unusual-month bell compare each month with the **2001–2022** mean of the same calendar month. A month needs at least 5 valid years to have a normal. The team lead chose this on 2026-11-15 over the full 2000–2024 record.

**Why:** NASA SVS 5478, the visual feed, averages 2001–2022. Using the same years means "normal" means the same thing in both workspaces. A judge comparing the two will not find two definitions.

**How to apply:**
- Don't change the baseline without documenting the decision and its rationale in [the mapping guide](../mapping-guide.md#the-normal).
- If the IMERG archive is extended past 2024, the normal stays 2001–2022. Only the ranks ("wettest of N") grow.
